1. Executive Summary

Convoi's advantage is community — Utah focus, early founder access, events, and LP relationships. The pilot turns that advantage into software.

Convoi Ventures has a structural advantage that most pre-seed funds cannot replicate: Utah focus, community density, early founder access, high-quality events, and deep LP/founder relationships.

That advantage currently lives in people's heads, Slack threads, Google Docs, event decks, and scattered recordings. It is powerful — but it does not compound at the speed Convoi is moving.

The opportunity: Turn Convoi's community advantage into software. A 30-day pilot builds Convoi OS — an AI-native operating layer for knowledge, founder workflows, dashboards, market maps, and AI agents.

What Trent gets in 30 days

  • Working internal Second Brain with searchable founder resources, playbooks, and event materials
  • 3–5 demoable AI workflows (meeting summaries, office-hours prep, investor intro drafts, cohort summaries)
  • Accelerator organizer and founder dashboard prototypes
  • Utah Hard Tech Week market map structure and hackathon ops prototype
  • Website modernization brief informed by real system architecture
  • A credible 90-day roadmap for what comes next

2. Core Thesis

Convoi should not just have a website or disconnected docs. Convoi should have an AI-native operating layer.

7. Demo Narrative

The story to tell Trent in the final demo.

Scene 1: Monday Morning Trent opens the organizer dashboard. 8 founders in cohort. Two flagged — one blocked on hiring, one demo-ready. Past notes, mentor feedback, and resources are already there. No Slack archaeology.
Scene 2: Office Hours Founder finishes a session. Recording uploaded. Convoi OS generates a summary, extracts action items, updates blockers, suggests resources, logs an investor ask. Founder dashboard updates automatically.
Scene 3: Investor Intro Trent needs to connect a founder with a SaaS LP. Investor intro workflow drafts a personalized email. Edit and send in 2 minutes instead of 20.
Scene 4: Hard Tech Week Defense-tech startup needs a manufacturing partner and lab connection. Market map filters by sector. Route to the right sponsors and hackathon judges.
Scene 5: Demo Day Cohort progress summary generated: milestones, demos, investor interest, blockers. LP update email drafted by the founder update workflow.
This is Convoi's community advantage, operationalized. The pilot proves it in 30 days. The 90-day roadmap scales it.
