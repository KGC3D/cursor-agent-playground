# Convoi OS / Convoi Founder OS
## 30-Day Pilot Proposal

**Prepared for:** Trent Mano & Convoi Ventures  
**Engagement type:** Independent contractor / LLC  
**Pilot investment:** $5,000  
**Duration:** 30 days  
**Date:** June 2026

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Convoi Ventures has a structural advantage that most pre-seed funds cannot replicate: **Utah focus, community density, early founder access, high-quality events, and deep LP/founder relationships.**

That advantage currently lives in people's heads, Slack threads, Google Docs, event decks, and scattered recordings. It is powerful — but it does not compound at the speed Convoi is moving.

**The opportunity:** Turn Convoi's community advantage into software.

A 30-day pilot builds **Convoi OS** — an AI-native operating layer that helps the Convoi team organize knowledge, support accelerator founders, run events, and surface investor/founder opportunities faster. This is not a website refresh project dressed up as strategy. It is the internal operating system that makes everything else — the accelerator, Hard Tech Week, LP relationships, and public positioning — work better.

**What Trent gets in 30 days:**
- A working internal Second Brain prototype with searchable founder resources, playbooks, and event materials
- 3–5 demoable AI workflows (meeting summaries, office-hours prep, investor intro drafts, cohort summaries)
- Accelerator organizer and founder dashboard prototypes
- Utah Hard Tech Week market map structure and hackathon ops prototype
- Website modernization brief informed by real system architecture
- A credible 90-day roadmap for what comes next

**Investment:** $5,000 fixed pilot. Scoped, deliverable-based, demo-ready.

---

## 2. Core Thesis

Convoi should not just have a website or disconnected docs. Convoi should have an **AI-native operating layer** — a system where:

| Today | With Convoi OS |
|-------|----------------|
| Knowledge lives in folders and memory | Knowledge is searchable, tagged, and AI-queryable |
| Founder progress is tracked ad hoc | Cohort health is visible in one dashboard |
| Event prep is manual and repetitive | AI workflows generate briefs, recaps, and follow-ups |
| Hard Tech Week ecosystem is implicit | Market map makes startups, labs, investors, and sponsors routable |
| Website reflects where Convoi was | Public site reflects what Convoi is becoming |

**Convoi OS is not a CRM replacement or a full product build.** It is the pilot foundation: prototypes, workflows, and architecture that prove the model before Convoi commits to production infrastructure.

The thesis in one line:

> **Community is Convoi's moat. Convoi OS makes that moat operational.**

## 3. 30-Day Pilot Plan

### Week 1: Discovery + System Map

**Goal:** Understand how Convoi actually works today. Map users, data, and workflows before building anything.

| Activity | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| Stakeholder interviews | Trent + core team (ops, events, accelerator) |
| Asset inventory | Docs, founder resources, accelerator workflows, recordings, event materials, LP touchpoints |
| User mapping | Convoi team, founders, LPs/investors, mentors, Hard Tech Week participants |
| Data model | Founders, companies, LPs, investors, events, resources, milestones, asks, demos, recordings |
| Information architecture | Module boundaries, navigation, permissions concept |

**Deliverables:**
- Convoi OS Blueprint (system map + product architecture)
- Product map (modules, dependencies, build order)
- Data model (entities, relationships, example records)
- Clickable wireframe or dashboard mockup

**Demo value:** "Here is what we're building and why — grounded in your actual workflows."

---

### Week 2: Second Brain + AI Workflows

**Goal:** Make Convoi's institutional knowledge searchable and actionable.

| Activity | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| Knowledge base setup | Organize founder resources, notes, event docs, recordings, playbooks |
| Tagging & structure | Cohort, topic, event, founder stage, resource type |
| AI workflow build | First 3–5 production-quality demo workflows |

**AI Workflows (initial set):**

1. **Founder meeting summary** — Transcript/recording → structured notes, action items, blockers
2. **Office-hours prep brief** — Founder profile + past notes → 1-page prep for mentors/team
3. **Investor intro draft** — Founder context + LP fit → personalized intro email
4. **Founder update draft** — Milestones + blockers → investor-ready update
5. **Cohort progress summary** — All founder data → weekly team digest
6. **Event recap generator** — Event notes/recording → recap post + follow-up actions

**Deliverables:**
- Working internal Second Brain prototype
- 3–5 demoable AI workflows (live, not slides)

**Demo value:** "Ask Convoi OS anything about a founder, event, or resource — and watch it generate the next action."

## 4. Scope Control

### Included in $5K Pilot

| Item | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Working prototypes | Functional demos, not production systems |
| Mock or lightweight real data | Enough to feel real; anonymized as needed |
| Dashboard UX | Organizer + founder views |

| Website refresh strategy | Brief + wireframes, not full rebuild |
| Weekly check-ins | Progress, blockers, priority adjustments |
| Final demo + 90-day roadmap | Clear handoff and next-step options |

### Not Included (Unless Separately Scoped)

| Item | Why excluded |
|------|--------------|
| Full production auth / SSO | Requires security review, user provisioning |
| Full CRM replacement | Affinity, HubSpot, etc. have deep integrations |
| Full investor portal | LP-facing product is a separate engagement |
| Full website rebuild | Design, CMS, content migration = separate project |
| Deep backend automation | Email sync, calendar sync, payment flows |
| Legal / compliance infrastructure | Data retention, PII handling, fund compliance |

**Pilot principle:** Prove the model. Ship demos. Decide what to productionize together.

## 7. Demo Narrative

*Use this story in the final demo and in the Trent meeting.*

---

**Scene 1: Monday Morning — Trent opens Convoi OS**

Trent logs into the organizer dashboard. He sees 8 founders in the current cohort. Two are flagged: one blocked on a hiring decision, one demo-ready for next week. He clicks into the blocked founder — past office hours notes, mentor feedback, and suggested resources are already there. No Slack archaeology.

**Scene 2: Office Hours — Founder finishes a session**

The recording is uploaded. Convoi OS generates a meeting summary, extracts action items, and updates the founder's blocker status. Relevant playbook resources are suggested. An investor ask is logged. The founder sees their dashboard update with new tasks and mentor notes.

**Scene 3: Investor Intro — LP match**

Trent needs to connect a founder with a SaaS LP. He triggers the investor intro workflow. Convoi OS drafts a personalized intro email using founder context, LP expertise, and past interaction history. Trent edits and sends in 2 minutes instead of 20.

**Scene 4: Hard Tech Week — Ecosystem routing**

A defense-tech startup needs a manufacturing partner and a university lab connection. Trent opens the Hard Tech Week market map. He filters by sector, sees 3 relevant entities, and routes the team to the right sponsors and judges for the hackathon track.

**Scene 5: Demo Day — Cohort summary**

Convoi OS generates a cohort progress summary: milestones hit, demos scheduled, investor interest logged, blockers remaining. Trent uses it for the LP update email — drafted by the founder update workflow.

**Closing line:**

> "This is Convoi's community advantage, operationalized. The pilot proves it in 30 days. The 90-day roadmap scales it."
